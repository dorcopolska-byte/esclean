End of Tenancy Cleaning

End of Tenancy Cleaning in Milton Keynes

Moving home is stressful enough without worrying whether the property will be clean enough for the next tenant, landlord or letting agent.

End of tenancy cleaning is more detailed than regular domestic cleaning. The goal is not simply to make the property look tidy. The goal is to leave it clean, presentable and ready for inspection.

Who Is This Service For?

  • Tenants preparing to move out
  • Landlords getting a property ready for new tenants
  • Letting agents needing reliable cleaning support
  • Homeowners preparing a property for sale or rental

Why End of Tenancy Cleaning Matters

When a property is inspected, small details can matter. Dust on skirting boards, marks on doors, limescale in bathrooms, grease in kitchens and dirty floors can all affect how the property is viewed.

A detailed clean helps present the property in the best possible condition and reduces the risk of cleaning-related issues after move-out.

We Work as a Two-Person Team

End of tenancy cleaning usually involves more time, more detail and more areas than a regular clean. That is why working as a two-person team makes a real difference.

While one person focuses on kitchens, bathrooms and detailed areas, the other can work through rooms, floors, skirting boards and general presentation.

This helps us complete the work more efficiently while maintaining attention to detail throughout the property.

What Can Be Included?

Kitchen

  • Worktops cleaned and sanitised
  • Sink and taps cleaned and polished
  • Hob cleaned
  • Appliance exteriors wiped
  • Cupboard fronts cleaned
  • Inside cupboards cleaned where empty
  • Tiles and splashbacks cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms and Toilets

  • Toilets cleaned and sanitised
  • Baths and showers cleaned
  • Sinks and taps polished
  • Limescale reduced where possible
  • Mirrors cleaned
  • Tiles and glass screens cleaned
  • Floors cleaned

Rooms and Living Areas

  • Dusting of accessible surfaces
  • Skirting boards cleaned
  • Doors and handles wiped
  • Window sills cleaned
  • Internal glass and mirrors cleaned where accessible
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped where appropriate

Bedrooms

  • Dusting
  • Skirting boards cleaned
  • Mirrors cleaned
  • Wardrobe exteriors wiped
  • Floors vacuumed and cleaned

What Is Not Always Included?

Some tasks may require additional time or a separate quote depending on the condition of the property.

  • Oven deep cleaning
  • Carpet cleaning
  • Exterior window cleaning
  • Heavy mould removal
  • Paint, renovation dust or builder's clean
  • Very heavy grease or long-term neglect

We always prefer to be honest before starting so expectations are clear.

Do We Need to See the Property First?

Where possible, yes. End of tenancy cleaning depends heavily on the size and condition of the property.

A small flat in good condition is very different from a large house that has not been cleaned properly for months. A short viewing or photos help us give a more accurate quote.

Can We Guarantee Deposit Return?

No cleaning company can honestly guarantee that a deposit will be returned, because the final decision depends on the landlord, letting agent and tenancy agreement.

What we can do is clean the property carefully and professionally, focusing on the areas that are most commonly checked during move-out inspections.

How Long Does It Take?

The time needed depends on the property size, condition and required tasks. As a two-person team, we can often complete the work faster than a single cleaner while still maintaining a detailed standard.

For best results, the property should be empty or mostly empty before cleaning begins.

Why Clients Choose ES Cleaning Service

Clients choose us because we are reliable, careful and honest about what can be achieved. We understand that end of tenancy cleaning is often time-sensitive and stressful, so clear communication matters.

Our goal is simple: to help leave the property clean, fresh and ready for the next stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you clean empty properties?

Yes. End of tenancy cleaning is usually easiest and most effective when the property is empty.

Do you clean inside cupboards?

Yes, where cupboards are empty and accessible.

Is oven cleaning included?

Oven cleaning may be included or quoted separately depending on the condition of the oven.

Do you clean carpets?

We vacuum carpets as part of the cleaning service, but professional carpet washing may require a separate service.

Can you clean for landlords or letting agents?

Yes. We can support tenants, landlords and letting agents across Milton Keynes and surrounding areas.

Can you clean at short notice?

Where availability allows, yes. Please contact us as early as possible, especially near move-out dates.
